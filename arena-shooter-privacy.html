Privacy Policy

Effective date:

📱 Mobile Game 🔒 Offline Only 🚫 No Accounts 💾 Local Save Data

🎯 Arena Shooter is a fully offline, single-player game. We do not collect personal information, create accounts, or transmit your data to servers. All progress is stored locally on your device.

1) Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Arena Shooter developed by Rockshell Studios (Ghana). If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

2) Information we collect

Short answer: Nothing personal.

All game data is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Game progress: level completion, stars earned, high scores
  • In-game currency: coins, diamonds, XP
  • Unlocks: purchased skins, bullet types, shields, backgrounds, trails
  • Settings: difficulty preference, audio volume, screen shake toggle

We do NOT collect:

  • ❌ Names, emails, or any personal identifiers
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Device identifiers (no Advertising ID collection)
  • ❌ Contact lists or phone information
  • ❌ Any data transmitted to external servers

3) How we use information

We don't "use" your data externally. The local data exists solely to:

  • Remember your progress when you return to the game
  • Keep your unlocks and currency between sessions
  • Save your settings preferences

4) Third-party services

Arena Shooter does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

  • ❌ No Google AdMob
  • ❌ No Google Analytics / Firebase
  • ❌ No Facebook SDK
  • ❌ No crash reporting services
  • ❌ No social login or cloud save

The game runs 100% offline with zero network requests.

5) No accounts or sign-in

Arena Shooter does not have user accounts, profiles, login screens, or sign-in buttons. There is no way to "sign up" because the game doesn't need any of your information.

6) No multiplayer or chat

Arena Shooter is a single-player experience. There is no online multiplayer, no chat system, no leaderboards, and no social features. No data is shared with other players because there are no other players.

7) Data retention

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. We have no copies on any server because we don't have servers.

8) Data deletion

To delete all your Arena Shooter data:

  • Method 1: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Arena Shooter → Storage → Clear Data
  • Method 2: Uninstall the app — this removes all local save files
  • Method 3: In-game reset (if added in a future update)

No email request needed — you control your own data.

9) Children's privacy

Arena Shooter is suitable for all ages. Since we collect zero personal data, have no accounts, no chat, and no ads, there are no COPPA or GDPR-K concerns. The game cannot identify or contact any child.

10) Security

Since all data is local and never transmitted, the risk of data interception is effectively zero. Your save file is stored in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot access.

11) Policy changes

If we update this policy (for example, if we add optional cloud save or ads in a future version), we will update the effective date above. We recommend checking this page after major app updates.
