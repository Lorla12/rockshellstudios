Privacy Policy

Effective date:

📱 Mobile Game 📢 AdMob Ads 🚫 No Accounts 💾 Local Save Data

🎯 Arena Shooter is an offline, single-player game with ads. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Ads are served through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers for ad personalization.

1) Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Arena Shooter developed by Rockshell Studios (Ghana). If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

2) Information we collect

Locally (on your device only):

  • Game progress: level completion, stars earned, high scores
  • In-game currency: coins, diamonds, XP
  • Unlocks: purchased skins, bullet types, shields, backgrounds, trails
  • Settings: difficulty preference, audio volume, screen shake toggle

Via AdMob (handled by Google):

  • Device identifiers: Advertising ID (used by Google to serve relevant ads)
  • IP address: may be collected by Google's ad systems
  • Ad interaction data: impressions, clicks, and ad performance metrics

We do NOT collect directly:

  • ❌ Names, emails, or any personal identifiers
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Contact lists or phone information
  • ❌ Any gameplay data sent to our own servers (we have none)

3) How we use information

  • Local data: only used to remember your progress and settings between sessions
  • AdMob data: used by Google to display ads and measure ad performance within the game

4) Third-party services

Arena Shooter uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying in-app advertisements

AdMob may collect and use device data under Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Arena Shooter does NOT use:

  • ❌ Google Analytics / Firebase
  • ❌ Facebook SDK
  • ❌ Crash reporting services
  • ❌ Social login or cloud save

5) Ads (AdMob)

📢 We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in Arena Shooter. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and IP address to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your device settings and region.

You can control ad personalization through:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads — reset or delete your Advertising ID
  • Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID — opt out of ad personalization

6) No accounts or sign-in

Arena Shooter does not have user accounts, profiles, login screens, or sign-in buttons. There is no way to sign up because the game doesn't need any of your personal information.

7) No multiplayer or chat

Arena Shooter is a single-player experience. There is no online multiplayer, no chat system, no leaderboards, and no social features. No data is shared with other players.

8) Data retention

  • Local game data: stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall
  • AdMob data: retained by Google under their own data retention policies

9) Data deletion

To delete your Arena Shooter game data:

  • Method 1: Android Settings → Apps → Arena Shooter → Storage → Clear Data
  • Method 2: Uninstall the app — removes all local save files

For AdMob data: visit https://adssettings.google.com to manage your Google ad settings and advertising ID.

10) Children's privacy

Arena Shooter is suitable for all ages. The game itself collects no personal data directly. AdMob ads are served by Google, which has its own compliance measures for child-directed content. If you are a parent and want to limit ad personalization for a child, use the Google Ads Settings page or disable ad personalization in your device settings.

11) Security

Local game data is stored in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot access. Ad data is handled by Google through their secure AdMob infrastructure.

12) Policy changes

If we update this policy (for example, if we add new services in a future version), we will update the effective date above. We recommend checking this page after major app updates.
