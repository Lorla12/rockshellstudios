King's Rules Ludo

Ludo reimagined with unique King's Rules. Unlock special attacks on your first 6 — Back Kick, Line Kick, Home Kick — plus Prison, Bonuses, and Safe Tiles.

⬅️ Back Kick 📏 Line Kick 🏠 Home Kick 🚓 Prison 🎁 Bonus 🛡️ Safe Tiles 🌐 Online
👑 Download on Play Store 🖼️ Screenshots
🎲 Ludo 👑 King's Rules 🌐 Multiplayer 📱 Android

Key Features

  • Back Kick — Move backward by dice roll to capture enemies
  • Line Kick — Dash parallel onto enemy tokens to capture
  • Home Kick — Interact between home-path and board tokens
  • Prison — Captured tokens jailed; roll a 6 to release
  • Bonus — Earn bonus actions on successful captures
  • Safe Tiles — Protected zones immune to normal captures
  • Online Chat — Per-color messaging in multiplayer
