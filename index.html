King's Rules Ludo

Key Features

  • Back Kick — Move backward by dice roll to capture enemies
  • Line Kick — Dash parallel onto enemy tokens to capture
  • Home Kick — Interact between home-path and board tokens
  • Double Dice — Roll two dice per turn; double 6s grant two extra turns
  • Prison — Captured tokens jailed; roll a 6 to release
  • Bonus — Earn bonus rolls on successful captures
  • Safe Tiles — Star tiles immune to normal captures
  • Walls — 2+ tokens form a wall, blocking enemies
  • Smart AI — Prioritizes captures, releases, optimal advancement
  • In-Game Tutorial — Context-sensitive guided tour
  • Online Chat — Per-color messaging in multiplayer

🏢 About

About Rockshell Studios

Rockshell Studios is an indie game studio based in Dzodze, Ghana, led by Gadese Samuel. We build games with strong gameplay, clean visuals, and a focus on player experience — from tactical shooters to strategic board games.

Mission

Create fun, accessible games and improve them continuously through community feedback.

Our Games

  • Arena Shooter — Tactical top-down cyberpunk action. 30 levels, 7 enemy types, 4 boss fights, endless mode, player skins, bullet types, shields, arena themes, and trail effects
  • Morris Games — Classic strategy board game
  • King's Rules Ludo — Ludo with Back Kick, Line Kick, Home Kick, Double Dice, Prison, Bonuses, Safe Tiles, Wall system, smart AI opponents, in-game tutorial, coins & XP rewards, and online multiplayer with per-color chat
